# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS in a single page, no build step. Deployable as-is to Netlify or Vercel.

## Users

Two audiences with equal weight, nearly always on a phone, arriving from Google Maps or Instagram:

- Adults from Santomera and nearby who want to train boxing, jiu-jitsu, kickboxing or K1 (fitness, self-defence, competition).
- Parents looking for an activity for their children (Infantil, Benjamín, Junior, Cadetes groups) who care about discipline, respect and safety.

## Product Purpose

The website of Leonidas Sport Center, a martial-arts club in Santomera (Murcia) open since 2011. Success: a visitor understands what is taught, when, and where within seconds, and messages the club on WhatsApp to book a trial class.

## Positioning

A local fight club with more than a decade in the town, real ring and bag area, and classes for every age group under one roof. Its motto is "Fuerza · Respeto".

- Existing identity on the poster: black, red and gold; Spartan helmet logo.

## Product Principles

1. The trial-class message on WhatsApp is the one action that matters.
2. The timetable must be readable on a phone in seconds.
3. Speak to parents as directly as to fighters.
4. Only real facts: no invented titles, prices, reviews or member counts.
